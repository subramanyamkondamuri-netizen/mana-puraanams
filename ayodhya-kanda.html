Ramayanam · Kanda 2

Ayodhya Kanda

By Mr. K.V.V Subramanyam

RAMAYANAM: AYODHYA KANDA

Ayodhya was the capital city of Kosala Rajyam that had been ruled by the Ikshwaku Dynasty. Ayodhya city was built by Emperor Manu. The city was rectangular in Shape. The streets were straight, wide, and parallel, with flower gardens on Either side. The roads were watered daily to cool the atmosphere. The fragrance Of flowers and incense sticks was pleasant in the streets.

The king’s fort was built amidst the city. It was impossible for enemies to enter The fort (Satrudurbhedyam). The people were all rich and happy. They lived in Joint families. At least ten members, including one guest, used to take their Lunch or dinner at a time. There were no thieves. People wore good clothes and Ornaments. Happiness prevailed all over the country. There were no crimes. Justice was available to all. The king was rich, and the people were also rich Enough. Thus, the kings of the Ikshwaku Dynasty ruled the country to the utmost Happiness of all the people. The agricultural yields were huge, as there were Three rains every month.

Dasaratha Maharaja had ruled Ayodhya for sixty thousand years. One day, he Proposed to celebrate Sri Rama Pattabhishekam in a large meeting convened with All his Pandits, kings, ministers, elders of Ayodhya, and senior people of Kosala Rajyam. He announced his proposal to celebrate Sri Rama Pattabhishekam. All in the meeting happily accepted and said that they wanted to see Sri Rama as A king on an elephant and under a white umbrella. Dasaradha questioned why. They Said that Rama was Satya-Dharma Parayanudu; that he was able to keep up dharma In earning money and never went for illegal earnings to fulfil his wants (Artha To meet Kama). He looked like the moon to them. Though Rama knew so many Astras, He never misused them. Rama was as intelligent as Brihaspathi and as strong as Indrudu to safeguard his people. If Rama and Laxmana waged a war, they were sure To win. After such victory, Rama did not prefer to take rest, but to go to people On chariot or elephant to talk to each of them and enquire about problems.

Rama felt as happy as a father if the people enjoyed anything, even if he was not Able to enjoy it. So, people—young or old, men or women, known or unknown—used to Pray to the Gods in temples to give health and strength to Rama.

Hearing the unanimous public opinion, Dasaradha felt very happy and declared to Celebrate Sri Rama Pattabhishekam the next day during the Pushyami Nakshatram. Dasaradha himself fixed the time and date in that happy mood. He assigned all Works to his ministers and others. He sent messages to all relatives, kings, and Rishis, inviting them. All people were happy. People started decorating their Houses with colors and streets with rangavallulu and flowers. A festive mood Prevailed all over, and Dasaradha was the happiest. He went to his queen’s Palace, informed Kausalya, and shared the joy of Sri Rama Pattabhishekam. Then He shared the joy with Sumitra. And then he turned towards Kaikeyi’s Mahal with The hope that she would feel even happier, as she held a greater part in the Upbringing of Rama.

In the meanwhile, Manthara, the old personal servant maid of Kaikeyi, reminded Kaikeyi about the two boons assured by Dasaradha previously. She provoked Kaikeyi to make use of the opportunity by demanding Dasaradha to celebrate Pattabhishekam for Bharata (the son of Kaikeyi) and to send Rama to the forest For fourteen years. Kaikeyi was strongly convinced. So, she laid down on the Floor with loosened hair and a serious face by the time Dasaradha entered her Chamber.

The joyful Dasaradha saw the silent, serious, and lonely laid-down Kaikeyi. He Slowly asked her what happened or what she wanted, offering anything in that happy Mood of Sri Rama Pattabhishekam. But after hearing her two demands, he was Astonished and asked her whether it was a joke. She said that the demands were True and serious, based on his earlier promise. He could not bear it and fell into A chair. He begged Kaikeyi to reconsider, offering many alternatives. He tried to Explain that all arrangements were in progress and such demands did not suit the Situation. But she was adamant. He fell at her feet and wept. He fell senseless For some hours and later woke up. He continued weeping and begging her.

At that time, Rama went there to share his joy with Kaikeyi, his dear stepmother. Seeing Dasaradha in a sorrowful mood, Rama asked why he was so. But Dasaradha Could not open his mouth or eyes. So, Kaikeyi herself explained her demands owing To the boons assured by Dasaradha.

Rama at once agreed to go to the forest so as to keep his father’s assurance True (Satyavan). Rama told Kaikeyi that Bharata would come from his maternal Grandfather’s house, Pattabhishekam would be celebrated for him, and then Rama Would go to the forest. But she replied firmly that he should leave Ayodhya at Once, and that she would take care of Pattabhishekam for Bharata. Rama, in Obedience and without anger, accepted her word.

The matter spread all over Ayodhya. Suddenly, sorrow replaced happiness all Around. Dasaradha did not sleep throughout the night and sat at her feet on the Floor. He fell senseless several times due to depression. He wept all along, Begging Kaikeyi to desist from her demands. She remained adamant. He was very Much tired.

Sri Rama took the blessings of sorrowful Kausalya. He then went to Sita, who Said that she too would follow Rama to the forest. He refused, stating that it Was risky. She replied that she knew the arm strength of Rama. Laxmana also Followed based on the suggestion of his mother Sumitra.

They went to Kaikeyi Mandir to take the blessings of Dasaradha, who hugged Rama And said that he was bound to truth and entangled in the heinous demands of Kaikeyi. But Rama decided to uphold his father in truth. Kaikeyi gave nara cheerelu (saintly clothes) to Rama, Laxmana, and Sita. But Dasaradha refused such clothes For Sita, the daughter-in-law of the king, and provided good silk sarees and Golden ornaments to her.

Sumantrudu, the senior minister, and Vasista, the Pandit, scolded Kaikeyi for Her negativity against the desire of the king, lakhs of people, and near and Dear. But she could not be changed. Rama, Sita, and Laxmana got into the chariot Driven by Sumantrudu. Dasaradha and Kausalya ran after the chariot, crying aloud, “Rama, Rama!”. Rama, who could not bear to see his father like that, asked Sumantrudu to raise the speed of the chariot. Thousands of people of Ayodhya—men, Women, rural folk, and all brahmins—followed the chariot towards the forest.

During that night, all the people slept around Rama, whose mind was thinking About his father. In the midnight, Sumantra, Rama, Sita, and Laxmana slipped away While the people were sleeping and headed deeper into the forest.

At Ayodhya, the weakened King Dasaradha was taken to the palace of Kausalya. There, he breathed his last.

Rama’s chariot reached the river Ganga. Rama sent back the chariot with minister Sumantrudu. There, Guhudu, a devotee of Rama, met and hugged Rama, and helped Them cross the river Ganga on his boat.

Then Sita, Rama, and Laxmana went to Bharadwaja Maharshi Asram, where they were Advised to go to Chitrakuta Parvatam, a suitable place for their Vanavasam. So, They went there.

At Ayodhya, the body of Dasaradha was preserved in chemical oils. Vasista sent Messengers to bring Bharata from his maternal grandfather’s house so as to make Him king, because the kingdom should not remain without a king. Only after Arriving at Ayodhya was Bharata informed of his father’s demise and Sri Rama’s Exile. He heard from his mother that she was the reason. He detested her. Bharata Declared that he would go to the forest and bring back Sri Rama. He asked all His mothers, pandits, ministers, soldiers, and the people of Ayodhya to follow Him to request Rama to return.

In the meanwhile, Satrughna caught Manthara and dragged her, saying that he Would kill her. But Bharata stopped him, stating that he himself spared Kaikeyi Because Rama would not speak to them if they killed a woman. Satrughna at once Stopped in obedience to Bharata. Thus, he won over his anger and proved his name Satrughna.

With the three mothers and all others, Bharata and Satrughna started for the Forest to bring back Sri Rama. Kaikeyi, the contrite mother, was the first to Board her chariot. On the way, they met Guhudu, who initially doubted Bharata, But later understood Bharata’s pure love and respect towards Rama.

Then they went to Bharadwaja Maharshi Asram, where they were all given warm Hospitality and good food. Then they moved to Chitrakuta Parvatam. Seeing them All at a distance, Laxmana suspected Bharata might have harmful intentions; but Rama pacified him, saying that Bharata was a noble prince who would never cause Harm. Bharata, running fast towards Rama, fell down. Rama moved forward, lifted Bharata, and embraced him. They hugged each other in deep affection.

Bharata informed Rama of Dasaradha’s demise. Rama wept like a child. He asked Sita to bring the required items and walk in front while he and Laxmana followed To the river Mandakini to perform rituals for Dasaradha. It is our custom that The wife walks in front only for the death rituals of elders. They completed The rites solemnly.

Bharata then asked Rama to return to Ayodhya and take up the responsibility of The kingdom. But Rama refused, stating that Dasaradha ordered him to go to the Forest and Bharata to rule the kingdom. Bharata said that the kingdom was given To him, and he wanted to give it to Rama. Rama replied that doing so would Amount to a violation of Pitruvakya Paripalana. Bharata argued that Dasaradha, Due to unsound health, might have conceded to Kaikeyi’s boons. Rama maintained That once he committed to go to the forest, he must complete the fourteen years To uphold his father’s truth.

Rama declined the counsel of Jabali, minister of Dasaradha, standing resolute For truth. Lastly, Bharata took upon his head the golden padukas (sandals) Stepped into and out by Sri Rama. Bharata placed Sri Rama’s padukas on the throne Of Ayodhya to rule. Rama remained deeply immersed in memories of his father.

As that area developed an unpleasant smell due to animal movement, Sita, Rama, And Laxmana left and went to Atri Maharshi Asram. The Maharshi explained the Greatness of his wife Anasuya, who gave blessings to Sita. Then Sita, Rama, and Laxmana moved deeper into the forest.
