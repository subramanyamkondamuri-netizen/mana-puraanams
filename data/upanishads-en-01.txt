INTRODUCTION

Salute to mother, salute to father, salute to teacher.

The valuable speeches of Sri Paripoornananda Swamy also inspired me.

Sri Tridandi Chinna Jeeyar Swamy
Once, I could meet him at Rajahmundry. His narration of the Ramayanam was telecasted on Maa TV from
5:00 AM to 6:00 AM every morning. I used to sit before the TV, exercise, and take down notes on the
Ramayanam. I developed a strong desire to write the Ramayanam. But to my misfortune, the television
channel altered its daily broadcasting schedule.

Sri Chaganti Koteswara Rao Garu
As I was much disappointed, my son loaded the complete 42-day Ramayana discourse of Sri Chaganti
Koteswara Rao into my cell phone. I had the fortune of meeting Sri Koteswara Rao garu at Rajahmundry once.
I used to listen to a portion of the discourse over the phone at least ten times each day. I wrote the account in
Telugu on the left-hand page of a notebook and translated it into English on the right-hand page. I then
prepared bound English copy books with the ambition of passing this sacred knowledge down to
schoolchildren. However, the schools declined to accept them, citing institutional secularism—a great
disservice to the children.

Sri Garikapati Narasimha Rao Garu
I could never meet him in person, but I regularly listen to his discourses on YouTube. Once, he mentioned that
authentic translations of the Upanishads are made available at very affordable prices by the Ramakrishna
Mission, advising interested seekers to procure and study them. I procured them immediately. Because they
are difficult to comprehend, I studied each page ten to fifteen times.

Later, I traveled to the United States to visit my son. There, I continued my studies and rendered these
explanatory notes in accessible language so ordinary readers could assimilate them effortlessly without
abandoning the texts. I titled this compilation "Me – The God".

I express my deepest gratitude to the three revered preceptors above for directing my intellect toward spiritual
contemplation.                                                              — K.V.V. Subramanyam, Kondakumari

Basically, what is meant by God? Where does He exist? How vast is He? Were the Sun, the Earth, and the
other planets created by God, and if so, how? While modern scientific theories offer valuable perspectives, our
ancient Rishis explained these existential inquiries to their ultimate source thousands of years ago.

Modern science explains that a cosmic explosion known as the Big Bang occurred, ultimately condensing into
the Sun, the planetary bodies, and their orbital paths. However, empirical science cannot identify the primal
instigator or underlying cause of this event.

Through transcendental insight, our ancient seers revealed: God is not a limited anthropomorphic form. He is
an infinite, all-pervasive ocean of conscious energy spanning boundless space. The Sun, the Earth, and all
planetary bodies constitute what we call the Solar Family. Yet this entire solar system occupies barely one-
hundredth of the divine energy matrix. The Supreme Reality is hundreds of times vaster than our solar
expanse.

Because this divine presence encompasses and permeates all things, the ancient elders designated Him as
Vishnu—Sarvavyapi Narayana. God is the Almighty: Omnipresent, Omnipotent, and Omniscient.

God dwells as a subtle spark within each individual and simultaneously expands as infinite space throughout
the cosmos. Nothing exists that is larger than Him; hence, He is Omnipresent.

An engineer may lack medical expertise, and a physician may be unfamiliar with engineering. A person
typically knows only a few spoken dialects. Yet God comprehends all sciences, all arts, and all languages
simultaneously because everything originates within His being. Thus, He is Omniscient. All instruments of
power, cosmic forces, technologies, and living creatures exist within Him; therefore, He is Omnipotent.

When the Supreme willed creation within the primordial void, the original cosmic vibration—Pranava Nada
(OM)—resounded with supreme force. Lord Shiva emerged as the primal kinetic potency, preceding physical
creation and transcending chronological time.

Lord Shiva exhaled a cosmic breath ('Uff'). From that breath arose fire; from fire emerged water; from water
the Sun condensed; and from the Sun, the Earth and the planets formed. The Moon detached from the Earth
and began its orbit. This unfolding represents the reality behind the modern concept of the Big Bang.

Thus, the five great elements—the Pancha Bhutas (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space)—were established.
Energized by solar radiance, life appeared: first aquatic organisms, followed by plant life, animals, birds, and
insects. After eons, human beings emerged, fulfilling the divine design.

Under divine will, Lord Brahma conducted the detailed manifestation of species, forming 8,400,000 life-
forms. An individual soul attains human birth only after evolving through these countless biological states. By
living a life of virtue, moral integrity, and devotion, the soul attains the supreme opportunity to merge back
into God.

The greatest endowment granted to mankind is intellect. Using intellect, man observed birds and developed
articulate speech; he observed their nests and learned to construct shelters; he observed wildlife and
discovered food, water, and family cohesion.
